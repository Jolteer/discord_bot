FAQ

Questions admins ask first

Which permissions does JoltBot ask for? {{ i.icon("plus") }}
Only the ones it uses: View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files, Read Message History, Manage Channels, Manage Roles, Manage Messages, Pin Messages, Mention All Roles and Kick Members. Kick Members is used only when your staff choose to deny and kick. It never asks for Administrator.
Who can change the settings? {{ i.icon("plus") }}
The server owner and members with Manage Server or Administrator. You can also let chosen roles, such as your moderators, use the dashboard. Every change is recorded in the audit log with who made it.
Where do transcripts go? {{ i.icon("plus") }}
To a log channel you pick in your own server. When a ticket closes, JoltBot posts the transcript and the decision there, and can copy uploaded files across before the ticket channel is deleted.
Can I change what JoltBot says? {{ i.icon("plus") }}
Yes. The panel, the form, the DMs and every reply are yours to write, with placeholders such as {user} and {server}. A live preview shows each message the way Discord will display it.
What happens if JoltBot goes offline? {{ i.icon("plus") }}
The dashboard shows a warning. Buttons and commands in Discord won't respond until it's back, and settings you save in the meantime apply as soon as it returns.
What if I remove JoltBot from my server? {{ i.icon("plus") }}
Your settings are kept for 30 days in case you invite it back. After that they are deleted, together with that server's ticket records and audit log.
